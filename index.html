01 — ABOUT

A little about me.

The person behind the keyboard, the curiosity behind the work, and the mindset I bring to engineering.

I'm Jeffrey Chandler Walsh, though most people call me Jeff. I enjoy the craft of software engineering and the process of turning an idea into something people can actually use.

I’m drawn to clear interfaces, dependable systems, and the small technical decisions that make products feel effortless. I believe good engineering balances curiosity with discipline: understand the problem, keep the solution thoughtful, and care about the details.

This site is where I’ll share selected projects, the tools I work with, and a little more about my path as an engineer.
